What is a SIP Calculator?

A SIP Calculator is a financial planning tool that helps investors estimate the future value of their investments made through a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP). It calculates expected returns based on monthly investment amount, duration, and expected return rate.

SIP investing is one of the most popular wealth-building strategies in India because it encourages disciplined investing and benefits from long-term compounding.

What is SIP?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly into mutual funds instead of investing a lump sum amount.

It helps investors reduce risk through rupee cost averaging and builds wealth over time.

How SIP Calculator Works

The SIP Calculator uses a compound interest-based formula to estimate future returns. It considers monthly investment, expected return rate, and duration.

SIP Formula

M = P × [((1 + i)^n − 1) / i] × (1 + i)

  • M = Maturity Amount
  • P = Monthly SIP
  • i = Monthly Interest Rate
  • n = Number of Months

Example

If you invest ₹5,000 per month for 20 years at 12% return:

  • Total Investment: ₹12,00,000
  • Estimated Value: ₹49,00,000+
  • Profit: ₹37,00,000+
