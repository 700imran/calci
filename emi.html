What is EMI Calculator?

An EMI Calculator is a financial tool that helps you calculate your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) for any type of loan including home loan, personal loan, car loan, education loan, or business loan.

It instantly shows your monthly repayment amount, total interest payable, and total repayment value, helping you plan your finances before taking a loan.

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is a fixed monthly payment made by a borrower to repay a loan. Each EMI includes both principal repayment and interest charged by the lender.

In early years, EMI consists mostly of interest, while later it shifts towards principal repayment.

EMI Formula

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1]

  • P = Loan Amount
  • R = Monthly Interest Rate
  • N = Loan Tenure in Months
