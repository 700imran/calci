What is GST?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is an indirect tax implemented in India on July 1, 2017. It replaced multiple indirect taxes and created a unified taxation system throughout the country.

GST is classified into:

  • CGST (Central Goods and Services Tax)
  • SGST (State Goods and Services Tax)
  • IGST (Integrated Goods and Services Tax)
  • UTGST (Union Territory Goods and Services Tax)

Common GST slabs in India include 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Our GST Calculator India supports all major GST rates used across industries.

How Does the GST Calculator Work?

The GST Tax Calculator applies the selected GST percentage to the taxable value or extracts the tax amount from an inclusive price.

GST Exclusive Calculation

Use this method when GST needs to be added to the original price.

Formula:

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

GST Inclusive Calculation

Use this method when the entered amount already includes GST.

Formula:

GST Amount = Inclusive Amount × GST Rate ÷ (100 + GST Rate)

Original Amount = Inclusive Amount − GST Amount

GST Calculation Example

Suppose the product value is ₹10,000 and the GST rate is 18%.

GST Amount = ₹10,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹1,800

Total Price = ₹10,000 + ₹1,800 = ₹11,800

If ₹11,800 is already GST-inclusive:

GST Amount = ₹11,800 × 18 ÷ 118 = ₹1,800

Taxable Value = ₹10,000
