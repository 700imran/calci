What is a Percentage Calculator?

A Percentage Calculator is a simple yet powerful online tool that helps you calculate percentages quickly and accurately. Whether you want to find the percentage of a number, calculate percentage increase or decrease, or determine marks percentage, this calculator makes the process fast and error-free.

Percentages are widely used in everyday life including academics, finance, business, shopping discounts, and statistics.

What is Percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. It is represented by the symbol %. For example, 50% means 50 out of 100.

  • Exam marks percentage
  • Shopping discounts
  • Profit & loss calculation
  • Bank interest rates
  • Data analysis

Percentage Formula

(Part / Whole) × 100

Percentage Increase & Decrease

Increase: (New - Old) / Old × 100

Decrease: (Old - New) / Old × 100
