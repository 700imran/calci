What is Gratuity Calculator?

A Gratuity Calculator is a financial tool that helps employees calculate the gratuity amount they are eligible to receive after completing a certain number of years in an organization.

It simplifies complex gratuity formulas and provides instant estimation based on salary and service duration.

What is Gratuity?

Gratuity is a lump sum benefit paid by an employer to an employee as a reward for long-term service. It is governed by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 in India.

It is usually paid at retirement, resignation, or in case of death or disability.

Gratuity Formula

(Last Drawn Salary × 15 × Years of Service) ÷ 26

  • Salary = Basic + Dearness Allowance
  • 15 = 15 days salary per year
  • 26 = Working days in a month

Example Calculation

Salary = ₹50,000
Service = 10 years

  • Gratuity = (50,000 × 15 × 10) ÷ 26
  • ≈ ₹2,88,461

How Gratuity Calculator Works

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 5 years of continuous service (generally)
  • Applicable under Gratuity Act, 1972
  • Retirement, resignation, or termination cases
  • Relaxation in case of death or disability

Benefits of Gratuity Calculator

  • Instant gratuity estimation
  • Helps retirement planning
  • Shows eligibility status
  • Accurate and error-free results
  • Useful for job change decisions

Why Gratuity is Important

Gratuity provides financial security after long-term employment and acts as a reward for employee loyalty.

It is a key component of retirement benefits along with EPF and pension.

Conclusion

A Gratuity Calculator is an essential tool for employees to estimate their end-of-service benefits. It ensures transparency, simplifies calculations, and helps in better financial planning.
